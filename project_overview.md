Flood Detection System Using IoT

1. Project Overview

The Flood Detection System using Internet of Things (IoT) is designed to monitor water levels and identify potential flood situations.

The system uses sensors and a microcontroller to detect rising water levels and provide alerts when the water reaches a dangerous level.

2. Objectives

- Monitor water levels continuously.
- Detect possible flood conditions.
- Provide alerts during high water levels.
- Reduce the risk of damage to people and property.

3. Technologies Used

- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Embedded Systems
- Arduino / ESP8266 / ESP32
- Water Level Sensor
- Buzzer and Alert System

4. Working Principle

1. The water level sensor measures the water level.
2. The microcontroller receives sensor readings.
3. The readings are compared with predefined threshold values.
4. When the water level becomes critical, an alert is activated.
5. The system can be extended to send notifications through an IoT platform.

5. Applications

- Flood-prone areas
- Rivers and dams
- Agricultural fields
- Smart cities
- Water management systems

6. Expected Outcome

An IoT-based monitoring system that helps detect rising water levels and provides early warnings to reduce flood-related risks.

7. Future Enhancements

- Mobile notifications
- Cloud-based monitoring
- Real-time data visualization
- Rainfall monitoring integration
